🏃 The Big Race
Leo trained every morning for the school race. On race day, he felt nervous but excited. When the whistle blew, Leo ran as fast as he could. He did not come first, but he finished the race with a big smile. His friends cheered loudly for him at the finish line.
  1. What did Leo do every morning?
    A) TrainedB) Slept inC) Watched TVD) Read books
  2. How did Leo feel before the race?
    A) Nervous but excitedB) BoredC) AngryD) Sleepy
  3. Did Leo come first in the race?
    A) NoB) YesC) He did not finishD) He came second
  4. What did his friends do?
    A) Cheered for himB) Laughed at himC) Ignored himD) Left early

Answer key

  1. A) Trained
  2. A) Nervous but excited
  3. A) No
  4. A) Cheered for him
