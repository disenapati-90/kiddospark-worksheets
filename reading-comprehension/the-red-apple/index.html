Reading Comprehension: The Red Apple

Read the story, then answer the questions.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 4
🍎 The Red Apple
Lily found a big red apple under a tree. The apple was shiny and round. She picked it up and wiped it clean on her shirt. Then she took a big bite. It was sweet and yummy! Lily smiled. She decided to save the seeds and plant a new apple tree someday.
  1. Where did Lily find the apple?
    A) In a basketB) Under a treeC) In her bagD) On a shelf
  2. What did the apple taste like?
    A) SourB) SaltyC) SweetD) Bitter
  3. What did Lily do before eating it?
    A) Washed itB) Wiped it cleanC) Cooked itD) Cut it
  4. What did Lily want to do with the seeds?
    A) Throw awayB) Plant a treeC) Give awayD) Eat them

Answer key

  1. B) Under a tree
  2. C) Sweet
  3. B) Wiped it clean
  4. B) Plant a tree
