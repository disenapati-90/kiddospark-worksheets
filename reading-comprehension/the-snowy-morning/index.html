Reading Comprehension: The Snowy Morning

Read the story, then answer the questions.

⛄ The Snowy Morning
Ella woke up and saw snow covering the whole street. She quickly put on her boots and coat. Outside, she built a snowman with a carrot nose and two button eyes. Her friends came over and they had a snowball fight. Afterwards, they all drank warm hot chocolate together.
  1. What covered the street?
    A) LeavesB) RainC) SnowD) Sand
  2. What did Ella use for the snowman’s nose?
    A) A stickB) A carrotC) A stoneD) A button
  3. What did Ella and her friends do together?
    A) Read booksB) Had a snowball fightC) Watched TVD) Went swimming
  4. What did they drink at the end?
    A) JuiceB) MilkC) Hot chocolateD) Water

Answer key

  1. C) Snow
  2. B) A carrot
  3. B) Had a snowball fight
  4. C) Hot chocolate
