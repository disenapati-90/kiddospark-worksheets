🧦 The Missing Sock
Every laundry day, one of Milo's socks would disappear. He looked under his bed and behind the dryer. One day, he found his puppy Buddy playing with a pile of missing socks under the sofa. Milo laughed and gave Buddy a new toy to play with instead.
  1. What kept disappearing?
    A) A sockB) A shoeC) A toyD) A book
  2. Where did Milo find the socks?
    A) Under the sofaB) In the fridgeC) In the gardenD) Under his bed
  3. Who was taking the socks?
    A) Buddy the puppyB) His sisterC) A mouseD) The wind
  4. What did Milo give Buddy?
    A) A new toyB) A sockC) A treatD) A bath

Answer key

  1. A) A sock
  2. A) Under the sofa
  3. A) Buddy the puppy
  4. A) A new toy
