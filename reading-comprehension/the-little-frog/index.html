Reading Comprehension: The Little Frog

Read the story, then answer the questions.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 4
🐸 The Little Frog
A little frog sat by a pond. The frog was green and small. It liked to jump and splash in the water. One day, it saw a big fish. The fish swam away fast. The frog jumped back onto a big rock and smiled.
  1. Where did the frog sit?
    A) On a treeB) By a pondC) In a houseD) On a cloud
  2. What color was the frog?
    A) BlueB) RedC) GreenD) Yellow
  3. What did the frog see one day?
    A) A duckB) A big fishC) A catD) A bug
  4. Where did the frog jump at the end?
    A) Into the waterB) Onto a logC) Onto a big rockD) Up a tree

Answer key

  1. B) By a pond
  2. C) Green
  3. B) A big fish
  4. C) Onto a big rock
