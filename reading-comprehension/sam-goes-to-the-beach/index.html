Reading Comprehension: Sam Goes to the Beach

Read the story, then answer the questions.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 4
🏖️ Sam Goes to the Beach
Sam went to the beach with his family. The sand was warm and soft. Sam built a big sandcastle near the water. Then a big wave came and knocked it down. Sam laughed and started to build again. His sister helped him this time. Together they made the best sandcastle ever.
  1. Where did Sam go?
    A) To the parkB) To the beachC) To schoolD) To the zoo
  2. What did Sam build?
    A) A snowmanB) A boatC) A sandcastleD) A tent
  3. What knocked the sandcastle down?
    A) The windB) A big waveC) A dogD) His sister
  4. Who helped Sam rebuild?
    A) His momB) His dogC) His friendD) His sister

Answer key

  1. B) To the beach
  2. C) A sandcastle
  3. B) A big wave
  4. D) His sister
