Reading Comprehension: The Magic Garden

Read the story, then answer the questions.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 4
🌻 The Magic Garden
Nina found an old key in her attic. It opened a small gate behind her house. Inside was a secret garden full of colourful flowers. Butterflies danced above the roses. Nina visited the garden every day and watered the plants. Soon, the garden became the most beautiful spot in town.
  1. Where did Nina find the key?
    A) In her atticB) In the kitchenC) At schoolD) In a shop
  2. What did the key open?
    A) A small gateB) A treasure boxC) A doorD) A car
  3. What danced above the roses?
    A) BeesB) BirdsC) ButterfliesD) Bats
  4. What did Nina do every day?
    A) Watered the plantsB) Painted picturesC) Fed the fishD) Cleaned her room

Answer key

  1. A) In her attic
  2. A) A small gate
  3. C) Butterflies
  4. A) Watered the plants
