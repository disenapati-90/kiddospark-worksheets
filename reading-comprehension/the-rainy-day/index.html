Reading Comprehension: The Rainy Day

Read the story, then answer the questions.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 4
🌧️ The Rainy Day
It was a rainy day. Tom looked out the window. Big raindrops fell on the glass. He could not go outside to play. So Tom got his crayons and drew a picture of the bright sun. His mom put the picture on the fridge. Tom felt very happy and proud.
  1. What was the weather like?
    A) SunnyB) SnowyC) RainyD) Windy
  2. What did Tom draw?
    A) A catB) A houseC) The sunD) A rainbow
  3. What did Tom use to draw?
    A) PensB) CrayonsC) PaintD) Chalk
  4. Where did Mom put the picture?
    A) On the wallB) On the fridgeC) In a bookD) On the table

Answer key

  1. C) Rainy
  2. C) The sun
  3. B) Crayons
  4. B) On the fridge
