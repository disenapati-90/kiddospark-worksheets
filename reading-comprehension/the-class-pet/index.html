Reading Comprehension: The Class Pet

Read the story, then answer the questions.

🐹 The Class Pet
Room 4 got a new class pet, a hamster named Peanut. Every student took turns feeding Peanut and cleaning his cage. On Fridays, one lucky student got to take Peanut home for the weekend. The whole class loved taking care of their furry friend.
  1. What kind of pet is Peanut?
    A) A hamsterB) A rabbitC) A fishD) A bird
  2. What happened on Fridays?
    A) A student took Peanut homeB) The class had a partyC) School closed earlyD) Peanut got a bath
  3. How did the class feel about Peanut?
    A) They loved himB) They were scared of himC) They ignored himD) They forgot him
  4. What is the pet's name?
    A) PeanutB) BuddyC) MaxD) Nibbles

Answer key

  1. A) A hamster
  2. A) A student took Peanut home
  3. A) They loved him
  4. A) Peanut
