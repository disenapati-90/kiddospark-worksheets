Read the story, then answer the questions.

🪁 The Windy Kite
Jack made a colourful kite with his dad. On a windy day, they went to the field to fly it. The kite went higher and higher into the sky. Suddenly, a gust of wind snapped the string! Jack was sad, but his dad said they could make a new one together. Jack smiled and felt better.
  1. Who helped Jack make the kite?
    A) His momB) His dadC) His sisterD) His teacher
  2. Where did they fly the kite?
    A) On the beachB) In the fieldC) On the roofD) In the park
  3. What happened to the kite string?
    A) It got tangledB) It snappedC) It got wetD) It burned
  4. How did Jack feel at the end?
    A) AngryB) BetterC) ScaredD) Tired

Answer key

  1. B) His dad
  2. B) In the field
  3. B) It snapped
  4. B) Better
