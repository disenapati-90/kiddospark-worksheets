🐝 The Friendly Bee
There was a little bee named Buzz. Buzz flew from flower to flower every morning. She loved the sweet smell of roses and daisies. One day she met a butterfly. They flew together all afternoon. When the sun went down, Buzz went back to her hive and told all her friends about her new pal.
  1. What was the bee's name?
    A) BelleB) BuzzC) BessD) Betty
  2. What did Buzz love?
    A) Rain and mudB) Sweet smell of flowersC) Loud noisesD) Cold weather
  3. Who did Buzz meet?
    A) A ladybugB) A frogC) A butterflyD) A bee
  4. What did Buzz do at sunset?
    A) Went to sleepB) Told her friendsC) Ate honeyD) Flew away

Answer key

  1. B) Buzz
  2. B) Sweet smell of flowers
  3. C) A butterfly
  4. B) Told her friends
