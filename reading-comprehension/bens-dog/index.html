Reading Comprehension: Ben's Dog

Read the story, then answer the questions.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 4
🐶 Ben's Dog
Ben has a dog named Max. Max is brown and fluffy. Max loves to run and play. Every day, Ben takes Max to the park. Max chases the ball and brings it back. Ben gives Max a big hug. Max wags his tail and barks happily.
  1. What is the dog's name?
    A) SamB) RexC) MaxD) Buddy
  2. What color is the dog?
    A) BlackB) BrownC) WhiteD) Grey
  3. Where does Ben take Max?
    A) To schoolB) To the parkC) To the storeD) To the beach
  4. What does Max do with the ball?
    A) Eats itB) Hides itC) Brings it backD) Drops it

Answer key

  1. C) Max
  2. B) Brown
  3. B) To the park
  4. C) Brings it back
