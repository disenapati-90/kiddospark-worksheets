🚒 The Brave Firefighter
Mr. Sam was a firefighter in a small town. One day, a cat got stuck in a tall tree. Mr. Sam brought his big ladder truck. He climbed up carefully and lifted the scared cat down. The cat's owner said thank you with a big smile. Mr. Sam felt proud to help.
  1. What is Mr. Sam's job?
    A) FirefighterB) DoctorC) TeacherD) Farmer
  2. What was stuck in the tree?
    A) A catB) A birdC) A kiteD) A ball
  3. What did Mr. Sam use to reach the cat?
    A) A ladder truckB) A boatC) A bikeD) A balloon
  4. How did Mr. Sam feel at the end?
    A) ProudB) AngryC) SadD) Scared

Answer key

  1. A) Firefighter
  2. A) A cat
  3. A) A ladder truck
  4. A) Proud
