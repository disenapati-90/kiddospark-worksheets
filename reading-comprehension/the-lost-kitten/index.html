Reading Comprehension: The Lost Kitten

Read the story, then answer the questions.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 4
🐱 The Lost Kitten
Mia heard a soft meow near her garden gate. She found a tiny kitten hiding under a bush. The kitten was scared and hungry. Mia gave it some milk and wrapped it in a warm blanket. The next day, they found the kitten’s owner nearby. Mia was happy the kitten was safe.
  1. What sound did Mia hear?
    A) A barkB) A meowC) A quackD) A chirp
  2. Where was the kitten hiding?
    A) Under a bushB) In a boxC) On a roofD) In a tree
  3. What did Mia give the kitten?
    A) BreadB) MilkC) FishD) Water only
  4. How did the story end?
    A) The kitten ran awayB) They found its ownerC) The kitten got lost againD) Mia kept the kitten forever

Answer key

  1. B) A meow
  2. A) Under a bush
  3. B) Milk
  4. B) They found its owner
