Synonyms and Antonyms Worksheet 3

Circle the correct answer for each question.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 10
  1. Which word means the SAME as sad?
    A) JoyfulB) UnhappyC) ExcitedD) Proud
  2. Which word means the OPPOSITE of full?
    A) EmptyB) HeavyC) LargeD) Round
  3. Which word means the SAME as rich?
    A) PoorB) WealthyC) BrokeD) Plain
  4. Which word means the OPPOSITE of strong?
    A) PowerfulB) MightyC) WeakD) Tough
  5. Which word means the SAME as angry?
    A) MadB) GladC) KindD) Calm
  6. Which word means the OPPOSITE of wet?
    A) DampB) DryC) ColdD) Soft
  7. Which word means the SAME as joyful?
    A) SadB) HappyC) BoredD) Tired
  8. Which word means the OPPOSITE of old?
    A) AncientB) YoungC) WiseD) Broken
  9. Which word means the SAME as hard?
    A) SoftB) DifficultC) SimpleD) Easy
  10. Which word means the OPPOSITE of light (weight)?
    A) BrightB) HeavyC) ThinD) Small

Answer key

  1. B) Unhappy
  2. A) Empty
  3. B) Wealthy
  4. C) Weak
  5. A) Mad
  6. B) Dry
  7. B) Happy
  8. B) Young
  9. B) Difficult
  10. B) Heavy
