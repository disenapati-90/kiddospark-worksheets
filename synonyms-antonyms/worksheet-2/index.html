Synonyms and Antonyms Worksheet 2

Circle the correct answer for each question.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 10
  1. Which word means the SAME as begin?
    A) EndB) StopC) StartD) Finish
  2. Which word means the OPPOSITE of begin?
    A) StartB) OpenC) EndD) Create
  3. Which word means the SAME as afraid?
    A) BraveB) ScaredC) HappyD) Calm
  4. Which word means the OPPOSITE of up?
    A) HighB) AboveC) DownD) Top
  5. Which word means the SAME as quiet?
    A) LoudB) SilentC) NoisyD) Busy
  6. Which word means the OPPOSITE of quiet?
    A) SilentB) CalmC) LoudD) Still
  7. Which word means the SAME as tiny?
    A) HugeB) SmallC) TallD) Heavy
  8. Which word means the OPPOSITE of open?
    A) WideB) ClosedC) EmptyD) Full
  9. Which word means the SAME as pretty?
    A) UglyB) BeautifulC) PlainD) Dull
  10. Which word means the OPPOSITE of clean?
    A) NeatB) TidyC) DirtyD) Fresh

Answer key

  1. C) Start
  2. C) End
  3. B) Scared
  4. C) Down
  5. B) Silent
  6. C) Loud
  7. B) Small
  8. B) Closed
  9. B) Beautiful
  10. C) Dirty
