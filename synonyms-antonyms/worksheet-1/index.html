Synonyms and Antonyms Worksheet 1

Circle the correct answer for each question.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 10
  1. Which word means the SAME as happy?
    A) GladB) SadC) AngryD) Tired
  2. Which word means the OPPOSITE of happy?
    A) GladB) CheerfulC) SadD) Joyful
  3. Which word means the SAME as big?
    A) TinyB) LargeC) ShortD) Thin
  4. Which word means the OPPOSITE of big?
    A) HugeB) SmallC) GiantD) Wide
  5. Which word means the SAME as fast?
    A) SlowB) QuickC) LazyD) Still
  6. Which word means the OPPOSITE of fast?
    A) QuickB) RapidC) SlowD) Speedy
  7. Which word means the SAME as smart?
    A) CleverB) SillyC) SlowD) Weak
  8. Which word means the OPPOSITE of hot?
    A) WarmB) ColdC) SunnyD) Bright
  9. Which word means the SAME as little?
    A) HugeB) SmallC) TallD) Wide
  10. Which word means the OPPOSITE of day?
    A) SunB) LightC) NightD) Morning

Answer key

  1. A) Glad
  2. C) Sad
  3. B) Large
  4. B) Small
  5. B) Quick
  6. C) Slow
  7. A) Clever
  8. B) Cold
  9. B) Small
  10. C) Night
