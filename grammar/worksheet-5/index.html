Grammar Worksheet 5

Circle the correct answer for each question.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 15
  1. Which word is an adjective in "The tall giraffe ate leaves"?
    A) tallB) giraffeC) ateD) leaves
  2. Choose the correct contraction for "they are".
    A) TheyreB) They'reC) The'yreD) Theyr'e
  3. Choose the correct contraction for "is not".
    A) IsntB) Isn'tC) Is'ntD) Isnot
  4. Which word means the same as "small" (a synonym)?
    A) TinyB) HugeC) WideD) Loud
  5. Which word means the opposite of "open"?
    A) ShutB) WideC) LoudD) Empty
  6. Choose the correct plural: "There are five ___ in the basket."
    A) appleB) applesC) apple'sD) apples'
  7. Choose the correct possessive: "the ___ toys" (toys belonging to the dogs).
    A) dogB) dogsC) dog'sD) dogs'
  8. Choose the correct verb: "He ___ swimming every summer."
    A) goB) goesC) goingD) gone
  9. Which word describes a place in "We went to the park"?
    A) wentB) toC) parkD) We
  10. Choose the correct word: "This is the ___ cake I have ever eaten."
    A) goodB) betterC) bestD) goodest
  11. Choose the sentence with the correct word order.
    A) Blue is the ball.B) The ball is blue.C) Is the ball blue the.D) Ball blue is the.
  12. Which word is a proper noun?
    A) TeacherB) FridayC) cityD) dog
  13. Choose the correct verb: "The sun ___ in the east."
    A) riseB) risesC) risingD) rose
  14. Which word means the same as "look" (a synonym)?
    A) SeeB) HideC) RunD) Sleep
  15. Choose the correct word: "Neither the cat ___ the dog wanted to go outside."
    A) orB) norC) andD) but

Answer key

  1. A) tall
  2. B) They're
  3. B) Isn't
  4. A) Tiny
  5. A) Shut
  6. B) apples
  7. D) dogs'
  8. B) goes
  9. C) park
  10. C) best
  11. B) The ball is blue.
  12. B) Friday
  13. B) rises
  14. A) See
  15. B) nor
