Grammar Worksheet 4

Circle the correct answer for each question.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 15
  1. Choose the correct verb: "Tomorrow, I ___ visit my grandma."
    A) willB) wasC) didD) had
  2. Which word is the helping verb in "She is running"?
    A) SheB) isC) runningD) .
  3. What punctuation is used to separate items in a list?
    A) PeriodB) CommaC) Question markD) Exclamation mark
  4. Which sentence is a question?
    A) The dog is brown.B) Is the dog brown?C) The dog runs fast!D) Look at the dog.
  5. Which sentence is a command?
    A) Please close the door.B) The door is open.C) Is the door open?D) The door was closed.
  6. Choose the word that best completes: "The ___ cat sat on the mat."
    A) quicklyB) runC) fluffyD) jump
  7. What is the plural of "fish" (more than one of the same kind)?
    A) FishesB) FishC) FishsD) Fishies
  8. Which word is a common noun (not a special name)?
    A) LondonB) SarahC) cityD) Monday
  9. Choose the correct verb: "He ___ his homework already."
    A) finishB) finishedC) finishingD) finishes
  10. Choose the correct word: "There are three books on ___ table."
    A) aB) anC) theD) some
  11. Choose the correct verb: "Yesterday, she ___ a picture."
    A) drawB) drewC) drawedD) drawing
  12. Choose the correct verb: "They ___ football every Saturday."
    A) playsB) playC) playingD) played
  13. What is the plural of "man"?
    A) MansB) MenC) Men'sD) Manes
  14. What is the plural of "woman"?
    A) WomansB) WomenC) WomensD) Womyn
  15. Choose the correct word: "___ you like some juice?"
    A) WouldB) WasC) DidD) Is

Answer key

  1. A) will
  2. B) is
  3. B) Comma
  4. B) Is the dog brown?
  5. A) Please close the door.
  6. C) fluffy
  7. B) Fish
  8. C) city
  9. B) finished
  10. C) the
  11. B) drew
  12. B) play
  13. B) Men
  14. B) Women
  15. A) Would
