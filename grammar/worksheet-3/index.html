Grammar Worksheet 3

Circle the correct answer for each question.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 15
  1. Which is the correct comparative form: "This box is ___ than that one."
    A) bigB) biggerC) biggestD) more big
  2. Which is the correct superlative form: "She is the ___ girl in the class."
    A) tallB) tallerC) tallestD) more tall
  3. Choose the correct possessive: "That is ___ ball."
    A) SamB) SamsC) Sam'sD) Sams'
  4. Which word is a preposition (tells where something is)?
    A) UnderB) HappyC) RunD) Blue
  5. Choose the correct preposition: "The cat is ___ the table."
    A) underB) quicklyC) andD) but
  6. Which question word would you use to ask about a place?
    A) WhoB) WhatC) WhereD) Why
  7. Which question word would you use to ask about a person?
    A) WhoB) WhatC) WhereD) When
  8. Choose the correct contraction for "do not".
    A) DontB) Don'tC) Do'ntD) Dont'
  9. Choose the correct contraction for "I am".
    A) ImB) I'mC) I'amD) Iam
  10. Which word is the correct plural of "foot"?
    A) FootsB) FeetC) FootesD) Feets
  11. Which word is the correct plural of "tooth"?
    A) ToothsB) TeethC) ToothesD) Teeths
  12. Choose the correctly punctuated sentence.
    A) Wow that was amazingB) Wow, that was amazing!C) wow that was amazingD) Wow that was amazing.
  13. Which word means the same as "happy" (a synonym)?
    A) SadB) JoyfulC) AngryD) Tired
  14. Which word means the same as "fast"?
    A) SlowB) QuickC) TiredD) Loud
  15. Choose the correct verb: "Yesterday, we ___ to the zoo."
    A) goB) goesC) wentD) going

Answer key

  1. B) bigger
  2. C) tallest
  3. C) Sam's
  4. A) Under
  5. A) under
  6. C) Where
  7. A) Who
  8. B) Don't
  9. B) I'm
  10. B) Feet
  11. B) Teeth
  12. B) Wow, that was amazing!
  13. B) Joyful
  14. B) Quick
  15. C) went
