Grammar Worksheet 1

Circle the correct answer for each question.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 15
  1. Which word is a noun (a person, place or thing)?
    A) RunB) HappyC) DogD) Quickly
  2. Which word is a verb (an action)?
    A) JumpB) BlueC) TableD) Slow
  3. Which word is an adjective (describes something)?
    A) ChairB) SwimC) BigD) And
  4. Choose the correct article: "I saw ___ elephant."
    A) aB) anC) theD) none
  5. Choose the correct article: "She has ___ cat."
    A) aB) anC) someD) many
  6. What is the plural of "cat"?
    A) CatsB) CatesC) CatssD) Cat
  7. What is the plural of "box"?
    A) BoxsB) BoxesC) BoxiesD) Box
  8. What is the plural of "baby"?
    A) BabysB) BabiesC) BabyesD) Baby
  9. Which sentence ends with a question mark?
    A) I like catsB) What is your nameC) Run fastD) I am happy
  10. Which punctuation mark ends an excited sentence?
    A) .B) !C) ?D) ,
  11. Which word means the opposite of "big"?
    A) HugeB) SmallC) TallD) Wide
  12. Which word means the opposite of "hot"?
    A) WarmB) ColdC) SunnyD) Bright
  13. Choose the correct pronoun: "___ is my friend."
    A) HimB) HeC) ThemD) His
  14. Choose the correct pronoun: "Give the book to ___."
    A) SheB) HeC) HerD) They
  15. Which sentence uses the past tense correctly?
    A) I walk to school yesterdayB) I walked to school yesterdayC) I walking to school yesterdayD) I walks to school yesterday

Answer key

  1. C) Dog
  2. A) Jump
  3. C) Big
  4. B) an
  5. A) a
  6. A) Cats
  7. B) Boxes
  8. B) Babies
  9. B) What is your name
  10. B) !
  11. B) Small
  12. B) Cold
  13. B) He
  14. C) Her
  15. B) I walked to school yesterday
