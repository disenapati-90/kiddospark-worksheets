Grammar Worksheet 2

Circle the correct answer for each question.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 15
  1. Which word is in the present tense?
    A) PlayedB) Will playC) PlaysD) Had played
  2. What should every sentence start with?
    A) A numberB) A small letterC) A capital letterD) A question mark
  3. Which word is capitalised correctly for a city name?
    A) londonB) LondonC) LONDOND) londOn
  4. Choose the correct word: "They ___ playing outside."
    A) isB) amC) areD) be
  5. Choose the correct word: "She ___ a doctor."
    A) isB) areC) amD) be
  6. Which word joins two sentences together?
    A) DogB) AndC) RunD) Blue
  7. Which of these is a proper noun (a special name)?
    A) cityB) LondonC) riverD) school
  8. What is the plural of "child"?
    A) ChildsB) ChildesC) ChildrenD) Childies
  9. What is the plural of "mouse" (the animal)?
    A) MousesB) MicesC) MiceD) Mouse
  10. Choose the correctly written sentence.
    A) she like dogsB) She likes dogs.C) she likes dogsD) She like dogs
  11. Which word describes HOW something is done?
    A) QuickB) QuicklyC) QuicknessD) Quicken
  12. What is the opposite of "up"?
    A) DownB) LeftC) OverD) In
  13. What is the plural of "leaf"?
    A) LeafsB) LeavesC) LeafesD) Leaf
  14. Which word means more than one dog?
    A) DogB) DogsC) DogesD) Dogies
  15. Which word is a joining word (conjunction)?
    A) HappyB) ButC) JumpD) Green

Answer key

  1. C) Plays
  2. C) A capital letter
  3. B) London
  4. C) are
  5. A) is
  6. B) And
  7. B) London
  8. C) Children
  9. C) Mice
  10. B) She likes dogs.
  11. B) Quickly
  12. A) Down
  13. B) Leaves
  14. B) Dogs
  15. B) But
