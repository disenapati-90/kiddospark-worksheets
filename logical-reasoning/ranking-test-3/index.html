Ranking Test Worksheet 3

Think carefully and circle the answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 10
  1. Raj is in front of Sam. Sam is in front of Tom. Tom is in front of Uma. Uma is in front of Vik. Who is at the very back?
    A) VikB) UmaC) TomD) Raj
  2. 6 people stand in a line. Meera is 2nd from the front. Farah is 2nd from the back. How many people stand between them?
    A) 2B) 3C) 1D) 4
  3. A is older than B. C is younger than B. D is older than A. Who is the oldest?
    A) DB) AC) BD) C
  4. In a race, Farah finishes 2nd. Dev finishes right after Farah. Kabir finishes right after Dev. Who finishes 4th?
    A) KabirB) DevC) FarahD) Can't tell
  5. 7 books are stacked. The red book is exactly in the middle. The blue book sits right above the red book. What position is the blue book from the top?
    A) 3rdB) 4thC) 5thD) 2nd
  6. Priya is taller than Raj. Meena is taller than Raj. Raj is taller than Sam. Who is definitely the shortest?
    A) SamB) RajC) MeenaD) Priya
  7. In a queue, Aisha is 5th from the front and 6th from the back. How many people are in the queue?
    A) 10B) 11C) 9D) 12
  8. Sam is twice as old as Tim. Tim is older than Raj. Raj is older than Mia. Who is the youngest?
    A) MiaB) RajC) TimD) Sam
  9. In a line of 9 students, Aisha is 3rd from the front. Ben is exactly in the middle. Who is closer to the front?
    A) AishaB) BenC) Both the sameD) Can't tell
  10. A, B, C, D finish a race. B finishes before C but after A. D finishes after C. Who finishes last?
    A) DB) CC) BD) A

Answer key

  1. A) Vik
  2. A) 2
  3. A) D
  4. A) Kabir
  5. A) 3rd
  6. A) Sam
  7. A) 10
  8. A) Mia
  9. A) Aisha
  10. A) D
