Ranking Test Worksheet 1

Think carefully and circle the answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 10
  1. Ravi is taller than Sam. Sam is taller than Tim. Who is the tallest?
    A) RaviB) SamC) TimD) Can't tell
  2. Maya is older than Zoe. Zoe is older than Kim. Who is the youngest?
    A) KimB) ZoeC) MayaD) Can't tell
  3. In a race, Tom finished before Jerry. Who finished first?
    A) TomB) JerryC) Both the sameD) Can't tell
  4. 5 kids stand in a line. Priya is 3rd from the front. How many kids are in front of her?
    A) 2B) 3C) 4D) 1
  5. A is bigger than B. B is bigger than C. Who is the smallest?
    A) CB) BC) AD) Can't tell
  6. In a queue of 4 people, Rahul is 1st. Who is at the very front?
    A) RahulB) 2nd personC) 3rd personD) Last person
  7. Sam is heavier than Alex. Alex is heavier than Max. Who is the lightest?
    A) MaxB) AlexC) SamD) Can't tell
  8. 6 books sit on a shelf. The blue book is 4th from the left. How many books are to its right?
    A) 2B) 3C) 4D) 1
  9. Neha runs faster than Priya. Priya runs faster than Asha. Who runs the slowest?
    A) AshaB) PriyaC) NehaD) Can't tell
  10. In a line of 5 students, Karan is 2nd from the back. What position is he from the front?
    A) 4thB) 3rdC) 5thD) 2nd

Answer key

  1. A) Ravi
  2. A) Kim
  3. A) Tom
  4. A) 2
  5. A) C
  6. A) Rahul
  7. A) Max
  8. A) 2
  9. A) Asha
  10. A) 4th
