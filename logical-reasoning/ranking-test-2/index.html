Ranking Test Worksheet 2

Think carefully and circle the answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 10
  1. Aman is taller than Bala. Chetan is taller than Aman. Who is the tallest of the three?
    A) ChetanB) AmanC) BalaD) Can't tell
  2. In a race of 5 runners, Zara finished 2nd. Wins finished right after her. What position did Wins finish?
    A) 3rdB) 1stC) 4thD) 2nd
  3. Meena is older than Rita but younger than Sana. Who is the oldest?
    A) SanaB) MeenaC) RitaD) Can't tell
  4. 7 children stand in a row. Dev is exactly in the middle. What position is Dev in?
    A) 4thB) 3rdC) 5thD) 7th
  5. Sam is shorter than Raj but taller than Mira. Lining up shortest to tallest, who is in the middle?
    A) SamB) RajC) MiraD) Can't tell
  6. In a line, Anu is 3rd from the front and 5th from the back. How many people are in the line?
    A) 7B) 8C) 6D) 9
  7. Leo is older than Max. Max is older than Nia. Nia is older than Omi. Who is the youngest?
    A) OmiB) NiaC) MaxD) Leo
  8. A book sits 2nd from the top on a shelf of 6 books. How many books are below it?
    A) 4B) 3C) 5D) 2
  9. In a race, Priya beat Rohan, and Rohan beat Sam. Who came last?
    A) SamB) RohanC) PriyaD) Can't tell
  10. 5 friends stand in a line. Uma is 3rd. Vik stands right after Uma. What position is Vik?
    A) 4thB) 2ndC) 5thD) 3rd

Answer key

  1. A) Chetan
  2. A) 3rd
  3. A) Sana
  4. A) 4th
  5. A) Sam
  6. A) 7
  7. A) Omi
  8. A) 4
  9. A) Sam
  10. A) 4th
