🦁 Animal Kingdom for Kids

Key facts

  • 🐘 Mammals: Mammals are warm-blooded, have hair or fur, and feed their babies with milk.
  • 🦅 Birds: Birds have feathers, wings, and beaks. Most birds can fly.
  • 🦎 Reptiles: Reptiles are cold-blooded with scaly skin. They include snakes, lizards, and crocodiles.
  • 🐸 Amphibians: Amphibians like frogs and salamanders can live both on land and in water.
  • 🐟 Fish: Fish live in water, breathe through gills, and are cold-blooded.
  • 🐞 Insects: Insects have 6 legs and 3 body parts: head, thorax, and abdomen.
