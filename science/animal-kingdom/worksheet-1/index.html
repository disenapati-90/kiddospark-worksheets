Animal Kingdom Worksheet 1

Circle the correct answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 9
  1. How many legs does an insect have?
    A) 4B) 6C) 8D) 10
  2. Which animal is a mammal?
    A) FrogB) EagleC) ElephantD) Crocodile
  3. What do birds have that helps most of them fly?
    A) ScalesB) GillsC) FeathersD) Fins
  4. Reptiles are?
    A) Warm-bloodedB) Cold-bloodedC) Warm and coldD) Neither
  5. Where do amphibians live?
    A) Only in waterB) Only on landC) Both on land and waterD) In trees only
  6. How do fish breathe?
    A) LungsB) SkinC) GillsD) Nose
  7. What are the 3 body parts of an insect?
    A) Head, body, tailB) Head, thorax, abdomenC) Head, chest, legsD) Top, middle, bottom
  8. An animal that only eats plants is called a?
    A) CarnivoreB) HerbivoreC) OmnivoreD) Predator
  9. An animal that eats both plants and meat is called a?
    A) HerbivoreB) CarnivoreC) OmnivoreD) Scavenger

Answer key

  1. B) 6
  2. C) Elephant
  3. C) Feathers
  4. B) Cold-blooded
  5. C) Both on land and water
  6. C) Gills
  7. B) Head, thorax, abdomen
  8. B) Herbivore
  9. C) Omnivore
