Animal Kingdom Worksheet 3

Circle the correct answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 8
  1. Which animal group has NO backbone?
    A) MammalsB) BirdsC) InsectsD) Reptiles
  2. What do we call animals that are active mainly at night?
    A) Nocturnal animalsB) Diurnal animalsC) Hibernating animalsD) Migrating animals
  3. What is it called when animals travel long distances with the seasons?
    A) HibernationB) CamouflageC) MigrationD) Adaptation
  4. Which of these is a marsupial that carries its baby in a pouch?
    A) KangarooB) ElephantC) DolphinD) Owl
  5. What do we call a young kangaroo?
    A) CubB) JoeyC) KitD) Calf
  6. Which sea creature is actually a mammal, not a fish?
    A) SharkB) DolphinC) JellyfishD) Eel
  7. What is the largest animal on Earth?
    A) ElephantB) Blue whaleC) GiraffeD) Great white shark
  8. What do we call an animal that hunts other animals for food?
    A) PreyB) PredatorC) HerbivoreD) Scavenger

Answer key

  1. C) Insects
  2. A) Nocturnal animals
  3. C) Migration
  4. A) Kangaroo
  5. B) Joey
  6. B) Dolphin
  7. B) Blue whale
  8. B) Predator
