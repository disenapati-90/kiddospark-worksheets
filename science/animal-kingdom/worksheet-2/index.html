Animal Kingdom Worksheet 2

Circle the correct answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 9
  1. What is it called when an animal blends into its surroundings?
    A) MigrationB) HibernationC) CamouflageD) Pollination
  2. What do we call animals that sleep through winter?
    A) Migrating animalsB) Hibernating animalsC) Nocturnal animalsD) Aquatic animals
  3. A baby frog that lives in water is called a?
    A) CubB) TadpoleC) CalfD) Chick
  4. Which of these animals lays eggs?
    A) DogB) ChickenC) CatD) Cow
  5. What covers the body of a fish?
    A) FurB) FeathersC) ScalesD) Shell
  6. Which animal group includes frogs and toads?
    A) MammalsB) ReptilesC) AmphibiansD) Fish
  7. What do spiders have that insects do not?
    A) 6 legsB) 8 legsC) WingsD) Antennae
  8. Which of these animals can fly?
    A) BatB) SnakeC) FishD) Turtle
  9. What is a group of lions called?
    A) A packB) A prideC) A herdD) A flock

Answer key

  1. C) Camouflage
  2. B) Hibernating animals
  3. B) Tadpole
  4. B) Chicken
  5. C) Scales
  6. C) Amphibians
  7. B) 8 legs
  8. A) Bat
  9. B) A pride
