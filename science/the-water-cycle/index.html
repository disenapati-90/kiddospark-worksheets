💧 The Water Cycle for Kids

Key facts

  • ☀️ Evaporation: The Sun heats water in oceans, rivers and lakes, turning it into invisible water vapour that rises into the air.
  • ☁️ Condensation: As water vapour rises and cools, it turns back into tiny water droplets that join together to form clouds.
  • 🌧️ Precipitation: When clouds get heavy with water, the droplets fall back to Earth as rain, snow, sleet or hail.
  • 🌊 Collection: Fallen water collects in oceans, rivers, lakes and underground, ready to evaporate again and start the cycle over.
  • 🌿 Transpiration: Plants release water vapour from their leaves into the air, which also adds moisture to the water cycle.
  • 🏞️ Runoff: Some rainwater flows over land into streams and rivers instead of soaking into the ground — this is called runoff.
