The Water Cycle Worksheet 1

Circle the correct answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 12
  1. What is it called when the Sun heats water and turns it into vapour?
    A) CondensationB) EvaporationC) PrecipitationD) Collection
  2. What is it called when water vapour cools and forms clouds?
    A) EvaporationB) CondensationC) RunoffD) Precipitation
  3. What do we call rain, snow, sleet and hail falling from clouds?
    A) EvaporationB) CollectionC) PrecipitationD) Transpiration
  4. Where does water go after it falls as rain?
    A) It disappearsB) It collects in oceans, rivers and lakesC) It stays in the cloudsD) It turns to ice forever
  5. What powers the water cycle?
    A) The MoonB) WindC) The SunD) Gravity alone
  6. What is it called when plants release water vapour from their leaves?
    A) TranspirationB) EvaporationC) PrecipitationD) Condensation
  7. What do clouds mostly form from?
    A) DustB) Tiny water dropletsC) SmokeD) Ice only
  8. What is it called when rainwater flows over land into rivers?
    A) RunoffB) CollectionC) CondensationD) Evaporation
  9. Which state does water change into during evaporation?
    A) SolidB) LiquidC) Gas (vapour)D) Ice
  10. Why is the water cycle important?
    A) It makes clouds prettyB) It recycles Earth\u2019s water so we always have fresh waterC) It only affects the oceanD) It stops rain from falling
  11. What happens to water vapour as it rises high into the sky?
    A) It gets hotterB) It cools and condensesC) It disappears completelyD) It turns into rock
  12. Snow and hail are both forms of?
    A) EvaporationB) PrecipitationC) CondensationD) Runoff

Answer key

  1. B) Evaporation
  2. B) Condensation
  3. C) Precipitation
  4. B) It collects in oceans, rivers and lakes
  5. C) The Sun
  6. A) Transpiration
  7. B) Tiny water droplets
  8. A) Runoff
  9. C) Gas (vapour)
  10. B) It recycles Earth\u2019s water so we always have fresh water
  11. B) It cools and condenses
  12. B) Precipitation
