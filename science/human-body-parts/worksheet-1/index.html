Human Body Parts Worksheet 1

Circle the correct answer.

  1. Which organ controls everything your body does?
    A) HeartB) LungsC) BrainD) Stomach
  2. What does the heart pump around the body?
    A) FoodB) AirC) BloodD) Water
  3. How many bones does an adult human body have?
    A) 100B) 150C) 206D) 300
  4. Which organ brings oxygen into your body?
    A) HeartB) LungsC) StomachD) Brain
  5. The stomach helps to?
    A) Pump bloodB) Break down foodC) BreatheD) Think
  6. What is the largest organ in the human body?
    A) BrainB) HeartC) LiverD) Skin
  7. What do muscles help you do?
    A) Digest foodB) Move and liftC) Breathe onlyD) See
  8. How many senses does the human body have?
    A) 3B) 4C) 5D) 6

    A) EyesB) NoseC) EarsD) Tongue

Answer key

  1. C) Brain
  2. C) Blood
  3. C) 206
  4. B) Lungs
  5. B) Break down food
  6. D) Skin
  7. B) Move and lift
  8. C) 5
  9. C) Ears
