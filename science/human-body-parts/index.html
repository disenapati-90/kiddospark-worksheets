🧑 Human Body Parts for Kids

Key facts

  • 🧠 Brain: The brain controls everything your body does — thinking, moving, and feeling.
  • ❤️ Heart: The heart pumps blood around your body to carry oxygen to all organs.
  • 🌬️ Lungs: Lungs bring oxygen into your body when you breathe in and push out carbon dioxide.
  • 🥣 Stomach: The stomach breaks down food into smaller pieces so your body can use it.
  • 🦴 Bones: Your skeleton has 206 bones. They protect your organs and help you move.
  • 💪 Muscles: Muscles pull on your bones to help you move, lift, and even blink!
  • 🖐️ Skin: Skin is your body’s largest organ. It protects you from germs and heat.
  • 👁️ Eyes: Eyes let you see by sending light signals to your brain.
