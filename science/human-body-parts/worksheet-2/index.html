Human Body Parts Worksheet 2

Circle the correct answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 9
  1. What helps you chew your food?
    A) TeethB) HairC) NailsD) Skin
  2. Why is sleep important for your body?
    A) It makes you hungryB) It helps your body rest and growC) It makes you thirstyD) It has no purpose
  3. Which organ pumps blood and never stops working?
    A) LungsB) StomachC) HeartD) Skin
  4. How many bones are babies born with, roughly?
    A) Around 300B) Around 50C) Around 1000D) Around 206
  5. What protects your brain?
    A) SkullB) RibsC) SkinD) Spine
  6. Which body part pumps oxygen-rich blood to the rest of the body?
    A) LungsB) HeartC) StomachD) Kidneys
  7. What do your lungs do when you breathe in?
    A) Push out bloodB) Take in oxygenC) Digest foodD) Send signals to the brain
  8. What is the hardest substance in your body?
    A) BoneB) SkinC) Tooth enamelD) Nails
  9. Which organ filters waste out of your blood?
    A) KidneysB) HeartC) LungsD) Brain

Answer key

  1. A) Teeth
  2. B) It helps your body rest and grow
  3. C) Heart
  4. A) Around 300
  5. A) Skull
  6. B) Heart
  7. B) Take in oxygen
  8. C) Tooth enamel
  9. A) Kidneys
