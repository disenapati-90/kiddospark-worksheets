🪐 Solar System for Kids

Key facts

  • ☀️ Sun: The Sun is a star at the centre of our solar system. It gives us light and heat.
  • ⚫ Mercury: Mercury is the smallest planet and closest to the Sun. It has no atmosphere.
  • 🌕 Venus: Venus is the hottest planet and spins backwards compared to most planets.
  • 🌍 Earth: Earth is the only planet known to have life, liquid water, and breathable air.
  • 🔴 Mars: Mars is called the Red Planet because of its reddish iron-oxide surface.
  • 🟠 Jupiter: Jupiter is the largest planet. Its Great Red Spot is a storm bigger than Earth!
  • 💫 Saturn: Saturn is famous for its beautiful rings made of ice and rock.
  • 🌙 Moon: The Moon orbits Earth and causes tides. It takes about 27 days to go around Earth.
