Solar System Worksheet 1

  1. Which planet is closest to the Sun?
    A) EarthB) VenusC) MercuryD) Mars
  2. What is the hottest planet?
    A) MercuryB) MarsC) VenusD) Jupiter
  3. Which planet is known as the Red Planet?
    A) JupiterB) MarsC) VenusD) Saturn
  4. Which planet has rings?
    A) EarthB) JupiterC) MarsD) Saturn
  5. What is at the centre of our solar system?
    A) EarthB) MoonC) SunD) Mars
  6. Which is the largest planet?
    A) SaturnB) NeptuneC) JupiterD) Uranus
  7. How long does it take the Moon to orbit Earth?
    A) 7 daysB) 14 daysC) 27 daysD) 365 days
  8. Which planet is known for being icy blue?
    A) MarsB) VenusC) NeptuneD) Mercury
  9. What is between Mars and Jupiter?
    A) The MoonB) The asteroid beltC) The SunD) A black hole

Answer key

  1. C) Mercury
  2. C) Venus
  3. B) Mars
  4. D) Saturn
  5. C) Sun
  6. C) Jupiter
  7. C) 27 days
  8. C) Neptune
  9. B) The asteroid belt
