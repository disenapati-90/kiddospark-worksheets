Solar System Worksheet 2

Circle the correct answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 9
  1. What causes day and night on Earth?
    A) Earth orbiting the SunB) Earth spinning on its axisC) The Moon movingD) Clouds covering the Sun
  2. How long does it take Earth to orbit the Sun?
    A) 1 dayB) 1 monthC) 1 yearD) 10 years
  3. What is the Sun made mostly of?
    A) Rock and metalB) IceC) Hot gasesD) Water
  4. How many planets are in our solar system?
    A) 7B) 8C) 9D) 10
  5. Which planet is known as Earth’s twin because of its size?
    A) MarsB) VenusC) MercuryD) Jupiter
  6. What do we call a rocky object that orbits the Sun, smaller than a planet?
    A) A cometB) An asteroidC) A moonD) A star
  7. What is a shooting star actually?
    A) A falling starB) A meteor burning upC) A satelliteD) A planet
  8. Which planet is farthest from the Sun?
    A) SaturnB) NeptuneC) UranusD) Pluto
  9. What keeps the planets orbiting the Sun?
    A) WindB) GravityC) MagnetismD) Sunlight

Answer key

  1. B) Earth spinning on its axis
  2. C) 1 year
  3. C) Hot gases
  4. B) 8
  5. B) Venus
  6. B) An asteroid
  7. B) A meteor burning up
  8. B) Neptune
  9. B) Gravity
