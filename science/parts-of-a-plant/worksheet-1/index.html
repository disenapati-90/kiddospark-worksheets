Parts of a Plant Worksheet 1

Circle the correct answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 9
  1. Which part of a plant absorbs water from the soil?
    A) LeavesB) RootsC) FlowerD) Fruit
  2. What do leaves use sunlight to make?
    A) WaterB) SeedsC) FoodD) Flowers
  3. What is the process of making food in leaves called?
    A) RespirationB) PhotosynthesisC) PollinationD) Germination
  4. What does the stem do?
    A) Makes foodB) Absorbs waterC) Carries water and foodD) Makes seeds
  5. Which part protects the seeds?
    A) RootsB) StemC) LeavesD) Fruit
  6. A seed needs water, soil and __ to grow.
    A) WindB) SunlightC) Rain onlyD) Darkness
  7. Flowers help the plant to?
    A) Absorb waterB) ReproduceC) Make foodD) Store water
  8. What is it called when pollen moves from one flower to another?
    A) GerminationB) PollinationC) PhotosynthesisD) Respiration
  9. What is it called when a seed starts to grow?
    A) PollinationB) GerminationC) EvaporationD) Reproduction

Answer key

  1. B) Roots
  2. C) Food
  3. B) Photosynthesis
  4. C) Carries water and food
  5. D) Fruit
  6. B) Sunlight
  7. B) Reproduce
  8. B) Pollination
  9. B) Germination
