🌱 Parts of a Plant for Kids

Key facts

  • 🌿 Roots: Roots absorb water and minerals from the soil and anchor the plant.
  • 🌾 Stem: The stem carries water and food between the roots and leaves.
  • 🍃 Leaves: Leaves make food for the plant using sunlight — this is called photosynthesis.
  • 🌸 Flower: Flowers help the plant reproduce by making seeds after pollination.
  • 🍎 Fruit: Fruit protects the seeds inside and helps spread them to new places.
  • 🌰 Seeds: Seeds grow into new plants when they find soil, water and sunlight.
