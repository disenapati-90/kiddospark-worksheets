Parts of a Plant Worksheet 3

  1. What do we call a plant that lives for more than two years?
    A) AnnualB) BiennialC) PerennialD) Seedling
  2. Which part of the plant grows toward sunlight?
    A) RootsB) Stem and leavesC) SeedsD) Fruit
  3. What is the outer layer of a tree trunk called?
    A) BarkB) SapC) RootD) Petal
  4. What do we call plants that don\u2019t need soil, like some grown in water?
    A) Hydroponic plantsB) Carnivorous plantsC) Desert plantsD) Fungi
  5. Which of these is a carnivorous plant?
    A) SunflowerB) Venus flytrapC) RoseD) Cactus
  6. What helps hold a plant firmly in the soil?
    A) LeavesB) FlowersC) RootsD) Fruit
  7. What do we call the sticky powder inside a flower?
    A) NectarB) PollenC) SapD) Chlorophyll
  8. What is the green substance in leaves that helps make food called?
    A) ChlorophyllB) ChloroformC) CelluloseD) Carbon

Answer key

  1. C) Perennial
  2. B) Stem and leaves
  3. A) Bark
  4. A) Hydroponic plants
  5. B) Venus flytrap
  6. C) Roots
  7. B) Pollen
  8. A) Chlorophyll
