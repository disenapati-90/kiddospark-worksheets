Parts of a Plant Worksheet 2

Circle the correct answer.

Name: ________________Date: ___________Score: ____ / 9
  1. Which gas do plants take in to make food?
    A) OxygenB) Carbon dioxideC) NitrogenD) Hydrogen
  2. What gas do plants release during photosynthesis?
    A) Carbon dioxideB) OxygenC) SmokeD) Nitrogen
  3. Why do plants need sunlight?
    A) To smell niceB) To make foodC) To grow thornsD) To make noise
  4. What do we call a plant that only lives for one year?
    A) AnnualB) PerennialC) BiennialD) Evergreen
  5. Which part of the plant is usually underground?
    A) LeavesB) FlowerC) RootsD) Fruit
  6. What do bees carry from flower to flower?
    A) WaterB) PollenC) SeedsD) Soil
  7. What colour are most leaves and why?
    A) Green, because of chlorophyllB) Red, because of the sunC) Blue, because of waterD) Brown, because of soil
  8. What do we call the tiny holes on a leaf that let air in and out?
    A) StomataB) PetalsC) RootsD) Veins
  9. Which of these is NOT something a plant needs to grow?
    A) SunlightB) WaterC) TelevisionD) Air

Answer key

  1. B) Carbon dioxide
  2. B) Oxygen
  3. B) To make food
  4. A) Annual
  5. C) Roots
  6. B) Pollen
  7. A) Green, because of chlorophyll
  8. A) Stomata
  9. C) Television
